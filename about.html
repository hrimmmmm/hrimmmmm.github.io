Heather Hyerin Im
About

Heather Hyerin Im is an artist and researcher based in Seoul, working across artificial intelligence, moving image, and computational media.

Her practice explores the unstable space between human intention and machine generation, with particular attention to ambiguity, imperfection, and unpredictability.

Through generative processes, she investigates computational errors not simply as failures, but as aesthetic materials that reveal alternative ways of seeing and interpreting images.
