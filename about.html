Artist Statement

My artistic practice investigates how systems of representation and classification shape what we recognize as normal, correct, or meaningful. I am particularly interested in how certain ways of seeing, representing, and interpreting the world become accepted as norms, while others are treated as errors, exceptions, or deviations.

Working across generative AI, interactive media, and participatory processes, I explore these boundaries by placing familiar categories in unfamiliar contexts. From treating AI-generated errors as artistic material to constructing fictional genealogies and examining imperfections as evidence of human authorship, my projects create situations in which established distinctions become unstable.

Rather than correcting what falls outside established norms, I use ambiguity, contradiction, and unexpected encounters to make the assumptions behind these norms visible. Through my work, I seek to create opportunities for viewers to reconsider the categories through which they perceive and make sense of the world.
