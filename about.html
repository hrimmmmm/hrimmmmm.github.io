Hyerin Im
About

Hyerin Im is an artist, designer, and researcher based in Seoul, working with image, interaction, and computation.

Her practice focuses on the tension between human intention and machine-generated outcomes, exploring how control and creative agency shift between humans and machines. Her work has been exhibited at Daelim Museum, Seoul Auction, and COEX, and screened at the Austrian Film Museum. She received the Grand Prize at the G-Artience NEXT GEN 10.

She previously worked as an AR UX designer at Niantic and continues her independent practice as a creative technologist, with collaborations including Adobe, Samsung, Lotte, and Korean musicians.
